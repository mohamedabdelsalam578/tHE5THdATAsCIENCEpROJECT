## Dataset Description

These datasets contains Data about Vechiles flow and passenger flow all around cairo, Furthermore adding the employee access point relative to the population. Adding also the public transport methods (MicroBus, minibus Metro) and there stations.

## Why we Choose these datasets

We were thinking about the congestition problem in Cairo and the reason behind it, By analyising it to pin point the problem in regards of diffrent Point of views, and exploring hidden patterns.

## Questions

1. Is there a relationship between morning alighting counts at a stop and the job accessibility score (jobs_count_access_60mins) of its surrounding area?
2. Is there a relation between population density and the passenger per vehicle ratio at terminals? (Do high population areas with low ratio reveal a pattern of communities that rely on cars?)
3. Does people acually follow the drop points?
4. When does the diffrence between formal and informal arise. "When do people prefer the other".
5. The relation between population density and the terminals location, Does it matter?.
6. Does providing more routes means a higher passenger count?
7. Is there a realation between Morning and Evening boarding, while knowing the route?
8. Is there a relation between the boarding areas itself? "An area with a higher passengers count means also the neighboring areas have a high passenger count?"
9. Is there areas with very high populatiuon but low boardings? "And if there is, is it due to a pattern or deficiency".
10. What is the relation between the number of vehicles that return empty and the location of the terminal
11. What is the relation between terminals that are not used and the routes it provides
12. Is there a realtion between the vehicle type and the route length?
